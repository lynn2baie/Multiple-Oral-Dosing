Curves add up the exact single-dose (Bateman) curve for every dose given. At steady state the concentration within a dosing interval is F·D·ka/[V(ka − k)] · [e−kt/(1 − e−kτ) − e−kat/(1 − e−kaτ)], which gives Cmax,ss at its exact steady-state peak time and Cmin,ss at the moment of the next dose. Cavg,ss = F·D/(CL·τ); the dotted average curve is Cavg,ss(1 − e−kt). Triangles on the time axis mark each dose of drug A.
